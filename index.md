# TrackTalk Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** April 13, 2026

## Overview

TrackTalk ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use TrackTalk.

## Information We Collect

### Voice Data
- When you use TrackTalk to track conversations, the App processes audio from your microphone in real-time on your device.
- **We do not record, store, transmit, or retain any audio files.** Audio is processed in real-time and discarded immediately after analysis.

### Voice Enrollment
- On first launch, you record a 30-second voice sample to enable speaker identification.
- This voice embedding is stored **locally on your device only** in the App's secure storage.
- This embedding is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

### Session Data
- The App stores session metadata locally on your device:
  - Date and time of sessions
  - Word counts per speaker
  - Custom speaker labels (e.g., "John", "Mom")
  - Session duration
- **This data is stored entirely on-device and never sent anywhere.**

### No Other Data Collection
- We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, or any other personal information.
- We do not track your usage patterns or analytics.
- We do not require an account, login, or any sign-up process.
- We do not maintain any servers that store your data.

## How We Use Your Information

All data processing happens **on your device only**. We use:
- Your voice enrollment to identify speakers during conversations
- Session data to calculate word counts and statistics
- No data is used for marketing, profiling, or any other purpose beyond the App's core functionality

## Data Deletion

**Deleting the App:** If you delete TrackTalk from your device, all stored data (voice embedding and session history) is permanently deleted. This data cannot be recovered.

**Exporting Your Data:** You can export your session history as a CSV file via the Settings menu. This gives you a backup of your data in a standard format.

## Data Security

- All data is encrypted and stored securely on your device using iOS's built-in encryption.
- No data leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.
- We do not have access to any of your data.

## Third-Party Services

TrackTalk uses the following third-party libraries, all running on-device:
- **FluidAudio** (Apache 2.0 license): Used for speaker diarization. All processing is local; no data is sent to FluidAudio's servers.
- **Apple Speech Framework** (system framework): Used for audio processing. Apple's privacy policy applies to system-level operations.

## Your Rights

You have full control over your data:
- You can recalibrate your voice enrollment at any time via Settings
- You can delete individual sessions from your History
- You can export all your data at any time
- You can delete the entire app and all associated data

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Your continued use of the App following changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
